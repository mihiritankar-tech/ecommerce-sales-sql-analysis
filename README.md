## 📌 Business Problem

An online retailer that launched in **January 2024** sells across 5 product categories in 5 U.S. regions.
Leadership wants to understand:

1. How are sales growing, and is there seasonality?
2. Which **products and categories** drive revenue and profit?
3. Who are the **most valuable customers**, and do customers come back?
4. Which **regions and sales channels** perform best?
5. Where is the business **losing money** (returns, cancellations, discounts)?

## 🗂️ Dataset

A synthetic but realistic dataset (Jan 2024 – Dec 2025), generated by [`scripts/generate_data.py`](scripts/generate_data.py).

| Table | Rows | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `customers` | 800 | Name, segment (Consumer / Small Business / Corporate), city, region, signup date |
| `products` | 40 | Product name, category, unit price, unit cost |
| `orders` | 2,252 | Order date, sales channel, status (Delivered / Returned / Cancelled) |
| `order_items` | 3,452 | Product, quantity, price, discount per order line |

**Data model**

```
customers 1 ──< orders 1 ──< order_items >── 1 products
```

**Key metric definitions**
- **Net revenue** = `quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount)`, Delivered orders only
- **Gross profit** = net revenue − `quantity × unit_cost`
- **AOV (average order value)** = net revenue ÷ number of orders

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Strong growth with a heavy holiday peak
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total net revenue | **$354,735** |
| Gross profit | **$129,912** (36.6% margin) |
| Orders | 2,008 delivered |
| Active customers | 730 |
| Average order value | **$176.66** |

- Revenue grew **5x from 2024 ($58K) to 2025 ($296K)** as the customer base expanded from 213 to 671 buyers.
- **November and December generate 38% of annual revenue.** December 2025 alone brought in $68K.
- **January–February is the slowest period** (only 5.6% of revenue).

### 2. Electronics sells the most, but earns the least
| Category | Revenue | Margin |
|---|---|---|
| Electronics | $89.7K | **20.7%** 🔻 |
| Apparel | $80.0K | 47.3% |
| Office Supplies | $66.1K | 36.4% |
| Home & Kitchen | $61.2K | 31.4% |
| Beauty & Health | $57.7K | **52.4%** 🔺 |

- Electronics is the #1 revenue category but has the **lowest margin**. Apparel earns **2x more profit** ($37.8K vs $18.6K) on slightly less revenue.
- Top products: **Noise-Cancel Headphones ($22.4K)**, **Rain Jacket ($21.2K)**, **Paper Shredder ($17.5K)**.

### 3. A small group of customers drives most revenue
- The **top 20% of customers generate 60% of revenue**, a classic 80/20 pattern.
- **Corporate customers are only 12% of buyers but bring 36% of revenue.** They spend **$1,471 per customer**, 5x more than Consumers ($270).
- **38% of customers bought only once**, so there's a big opportunity to improve retention.

### 4. The Northeast leads; the Southwest lags
- **Northeast = 32.5% of revenue**, West = 24.6%. The Southwest contributes only 10.5%.
- **Website brings 55% of revenue**. Mobile App and Marketplace orders have a similar AOV (~$179).

### 5. Discounts and returns are eroding profit
- Margin drops from **39.8% with no discount to 25.3% at 20% off**.
- Returns and cancellations cost **$39.5K in lost revenue**. Home & Kitchen has the highest cancellation rate (5.2%), and Office Supplies the highest return rate (7.2%).

## 💡 Recommendations

| # | Recommendation | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch a Corporate / B2B account program** (volume pricing, account manager) to win more high-value business buyers | Corporate = 12% of customers, 36% of revenue |
| 2 | **Start a post-purchase retention campaign** (follow-up email + second-order coupon) to convert one-time buyers | 38% of customers never return |
| 3 | **Promote high-margin categories** (Beauty & Health, Apparel) in ads and bundles; review Electronics pricing and supplier costs | Electronics margin 20.7% vs 52.4% |
| 4 | **Cap discounts at 10%** except for clearance; use free shipping or bundles instead | Margin falls to 25% at 20% off |
| 5 | **Plan inventory and staffing for Q4**, and run a "New Year" promotion to lift January–February | Nov–Dec = 38% of revenue |
| 6 | **Investigate Office Supplies returns and Home & Kitchen cancellations** (product quality, descriptions, delivery times) | $39.5K lost revenue |
| 7 | **Test targeted marketing in the Southwest and Midwest** | Lowest-revenue regions |

## ▶️ How to Run

```bash
# 1. (optional) regenerate the dataset
python3 scripts/generate_data.py

# 2. build the SQLite database
python3 scripts/load_data.py

# 3. run every query and save results to results/query_results.md
python3 scripts/run_analysis.py
```

You can also open `ecommerce.db` in **DB Browser for SQLite**, **DBeaver**, or **VS Code** and run any query from the `sql/` folder.
Queries use standard SQL. Only the date functions (`strftime`) are SQLite-specific; PostgreSQL and MySQL alternatives are noted in [`03_sales_trends.sql`](sql/03_sales_trends.sql).
